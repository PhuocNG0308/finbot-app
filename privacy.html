Privacy Policy — Control Spending

Last updated: November 25, 2026

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app uses the Gmail API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly to read emails sent by banks and payment services to the owner's own Gmail account. It never sends, modifies or deletes email.

How the data is used

From each bank notification the app extracts the transaction amount, date, account and description, and stores them together with the cleaned email text in a private database on a server controlled by the owner. The data is used only to show the owner their own spending summaries and payment reminders.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising or to train AI/ML models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
